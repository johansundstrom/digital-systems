# LAB 1

## Install Arduino IDE

### PC 

* Choose ```Windows Installer```
* Follow installation procedure. Part of foftware will be installed at ```C:/Users/[YOUR_USER_NAME]/Documents/Arduino``` and will be referred to as ```sketchbook directory```

* https://github.com/espressif/arduino-esp32/blob/master/docs/arduino-ide/windows.md

### Mac

* Click ```Mac OS X 10.10 or newer```
* Follow installation procedure. Part of software will be installed at ```HD/Users/[YOUR_USER_NAME]/Documents/Arduino``` and will be referred to as ```sketchbook directory```

### Debian (Linux)

* Open Terminal
* Enter ```sudo apt update```
* Enter ```sudo apt install arduino```

* https://linuxhint.com/install_arduino_ide_debian_10/
